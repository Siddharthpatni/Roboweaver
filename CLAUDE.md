## References

- **headroom** (https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom.git) — context-compression
  layer for AI agents: compresses tool outputs, logs, files, and RAG chunks before
  they reach the LLM (reports ~20% fewer tokens for coding-agent workloads, 60-95%
  fewer for JSON/structured data). Ships as a library, a proxy, and an MCP server.
  Not currently integrated into RoboWeaver.
